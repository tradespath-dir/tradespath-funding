Financial supports for apprentices in British Columbia

WorkBC Apprentice Services Financial Support

  • Run by the BC Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills. It helps with costs during the in-school part of your apprenticeship.
  • It may cover basic living costs, new dependent care costs, daily commuting, travel to relocate for training, disability-related costs and living away from home.
  • Apply through WorkBC's Online Employment Services portal with the BC Services Card app or a Basic BCeID. After you apply you get an EI Reference Code to use when applying for Employment Insurance with Service Canada.
  • Unemployed apprentices can still apply if their SkilledTradesBC registration is active. SkilledTradesBC keeps registration active for 18 months after you lose your sponsor.

Loans for pre-apprenticeship and foundation programs

  • The B.C. Student Loan for pre-apprenticeship, through StudentAid BC.
  • The Canada Student Loan can also cover pre-apprentice or foundation training at a recognized institution.

Red Seal trades

Up to $4,000 per technical-training period through the Canada Apprentice Loan. The federal Apprenticeship Incentive and Completion Grants ended March 31, 2025.
