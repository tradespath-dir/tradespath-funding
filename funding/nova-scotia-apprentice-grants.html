Grants and supports for Nova Scotia apprentices

From the Nova Scotia Apprenticeship Agency (NSAA). Grants are taxable and paid based on available funding.

Apprenticeship Tools Grant: $1,000 (one time)

  • For first-time, newly registered apprentices who live and work in Nova Scotia and are employed full time (35+ hours a week).
  • You need 500 or fewer credited employer hours when you register, and you must apply within 90 days of the date on your Apprenticeship Welcome Letter.
  • Use it for new tools, clothing and protective equipment. Don't buy anything before you're approved.

Progression and completion grants

  • $750 Provincial Apprenticeship Progression Grant: in non-Red Seal trades, for each level completed except the last. In Red Seal trades, for completing Level 3, unless the trade has only 3 levels.
  • $2,000 Provincial Apprenticeship Completion Grant: for non-Red Seal trades with 3 or 4 levels, once you finish and receive your Certificate of Apprenticeship.
  • Deadlines: NSAA's page gives both 90 days and six months for the completion grant. Apply as early as you can and confirm with NSAA.

Employment Nova Scotia (ENS) help during technical training

The ENS Apprenticeship Program can cover some or all of your accommodation, travel, child care, books and, for virtual or blended training, a computer. You apply online through LaMPSS. Receipts are required for child care, books and technology, and books and technology can sometimes be paid in advance. ENS: 1-877-223-0888.

Also available

Red Seal apprentices can get the Canada Apprentice Loan (up to $4,000 per period of technical training, interest-free) and other federal supports.
