US Pell Grants for trade and career programs

Federal Pell Grant (2026-27)

  • Need-based grant for undergraduates; you don't repay it. You apply by filing the FAFSA.
  • Maximum award for July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027: $7,395. Minimum: $740.
  • Amount depends on your Student Aid Index, cost of attendance and enrollment intensity.
  • Lifetime limit: 12 semesters (or the equivalent).
  • Only at participating schools and eligible programs. Ask the school's financial aid office if its trade program qualifies.

New: Workforce Pell (from July 1, 2026)

  • Pell Grants can now cover short workforce programs: 150 to 599 clock hours (or credit equivalent), lasting at least 8 but under 15 weeks.
  • Each program must be approved by the state's Governor and by the US Secretary of Education, and must meet completion, job-placement and earnings standards.
  • Students with a bachelor's degree can still qualify for Workforce Pell (but not if they hold or are pursuing a graduate credential).
  • Workforce Pell students get Pell only, not other federal student aid such as loans.
  • Schools started applying for approval on July 1, 2026, so approved programs will roll out gradually. Ask the school directly.
