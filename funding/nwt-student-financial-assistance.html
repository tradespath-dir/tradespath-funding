Northwest Territories: Student Financial Assistance and apprentice supports

Student Financial Assistance (SFA)

  • For NWT residents in approved part-time or full-time post-secondary programs at designated institutions. You must be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident or protected person.
  • The Basic Grant covers tuition, books and travel. Per semester, Northern Indigenous Residents get up to $3,320 for tuition plus $875 for books; Northern Residents schooled in the NWT get up to $2,655 plus $700.
  • A Supplementary Grant or Remissible Loan provides a monthly living allowance. A repayable loan of up to $1,400/month is available depending on need.
  • Remissible loans can be forgiven by living in the NWT after your studies. You must apply for Loan Repayment Incentives by April 15 and October 15 each year; it isn't automatic.
  • Apply every academic year. You can apply before you're accepted to a program.

For apprentices: Skills Development Program (SDP)

SDP is commonly used for technical training and can cover travel, living allowances, tuition (up to $7,200), books and materials (up to $550), tools and clothing (up to $750), and childcare. Contact your regional ECE Service Centre. Apprentices may also use EI and the federal Canada Apprentice Loan.
