Alberta High School Apprenticeship Scholarships

  • $1,000 for Alberta high school students who are registered apprentices earning Registered Apprenticeship Program (RAP) credits, or who completed a Career and Technology Studies (CTS) apprenticeship pathway.
  • $2,000 (Bright Futures) if you did both RAP and a CTS apprenticeship pathway.
  • You must be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident or protected person (visa students aren't eligible), an Alberta resident by your MyTradesecrets address, finish your diploma requirements in June of your graduating year, and not have received a RAP/CTS scholarship before.
  • No application: consent to be considered in MyTradesecrets and keep your mailing address current. Recipients are notified, and cheques are mailed, by November 1.

Tradesecrets says more than 1,900 apprenticeship scholarships are available in Alberta each year, most on the same consent-to-be-considered basis.
