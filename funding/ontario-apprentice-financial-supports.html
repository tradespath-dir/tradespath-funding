Ontario financial supports for apprentices

Tools Grant: $400 to $1,000

  • A non-repayable, taxable grant for tools, protective equipment and clothing.
  • $1,000 for motive power trades; $600 for construction or industrial trades; $400 for service trades.
  • Eligibility: completed or exempted from Level 1 in-class training on or after April 1, 2020; a registered training agreement; registered as an apprentice for at least 12 months.
  • Eligible apprentices get an email with an application link, or can apply online (18+; under 18 uses a paper form signed with a parent or guardian).

Apprentice Development Benefit

  • For apprentices registered with Skilled Trades Ontario attending full-time in-class training.
  • Can cover living costs (up to $500/week if you don't qualify for EI or your EI is under $500/week), dependent care, commuting (if you live more than 24 km from the school), living away from home and disability-related costs.

Apprenticeship Completion Bonus: $2,000

  • Taxable grant for people out of high school who complete a non-Red Seal trade (no exam, or a provincial-only exam).
  • Apply within 180 days of your Certificate of Apprenticeship or Qualification. Skilled Trades Ontario sends the application.
  • Red Seal trades: see the federal $5,000 Red Seal Completion Bonus and Canada Apprentice Loan.

Tip: Ontario contacts apprentices mainly by email, so keep your contact details current with Skilled Trades Ontario. Questions: Employment Ontario, 1-800-387-5656.
