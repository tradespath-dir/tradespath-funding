Oregon Child Care for Construction (CCC)

Run by the Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries (BOLI) and Oregon Business Development, this program pays child-care subsidies for construction apprentices.

Who qualifies

  • Apprentices registered with the State of Oregon in a construction trade, or new journey workers who completed a registered apprenticeship in the past 5 years.
  • With children under 12 (or under 17 with special needs) at home who need care from someone other than a parent.
  • Who spend more than 7% of household income on child care.

What you get

  • A subsidy that keeps your out-of-pocket child-care cost at or below 7% of household income. For example, if your household earns $10,000 a month, you pay no more than $700 a month.
  • There is no maximum household income.
  • The provider can be a licensed facility or an approved family member, friend or neighbour.
  • BOLI says it also provides hardship assistance, work tools, personal protective equipment and more for registered apprentices.

How to apply

Applications go through BOLI's partner organization. Contact details, including Spanish-language help, are on the official page below.
