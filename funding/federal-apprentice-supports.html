Federal money for apprentices in Canada

Red Seal Completion Bonus (new): $5,000

  • For journeypersons who complete an apprenticeship and get certified in an eligible Red Seal trade designated in their province or territory, on or after April 1, 2025.
  • $5,000 per Red Seal certification, up to $50,000.
  • You must be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident or protected person, and not attending high school.
  • Applications open November 16, 2026. If you certified between April 1, 2025 and November 15, 2026, apply by May 31, 2027. Later certifications have 12 months from the certification date. Supporting documents are due within 28 days of starting the application.
  • Have these ready: your SIN, banking details for direct deposit, your trade and certification date, your province or territory, and your apprentice ID number.

Canada Apprentice Loan

  • Up to $4,000 interest-free for each period of technical training in a designated Red Seal trade (up to $20,000 in total, according to ESDC).
  • No payments and no interest until you finish or leave your apprenticeship training.
  • You can apply up to 3 months before training starts. Not available in Quebec, which has its own support.

Closed: don't apply

The Apprenticeship Incentive Grant ($1,000 per level, max $2,000) and the Apprenticeship Completion Grant ($2,000) closed on March 31, 2025. Many older websites still list them.

Also

Eligible apprentices can get Employment Insurance benefits while away at full-time technical training.
