Canada Student Grant for full-time students

  • 2026-27: up to $4,200 per school year ($525 per month of study). This grant doesn't need to be repaid.
  • No separate application: you're assessed automatically when you apply for student aid through your province or territory.
  • You need financial need, full-time enrolment in a qualifying program at a designated school, and family income under the threshold. For example, a one-person family gets the maximum below $38,474 and nothing at $69,987 or more (thresholds effective August 1, 2026).
  • Trades students, watch out: the federal regulations require a program of at least two years' duration, so short certificate programs may not qualify. Most students at private for-profit schools are no longer eligible, apart from exempted programs (nursing, dental hygiene, early childhood education and paramedic in 2026-27).
  • Not available to students from Quebec, the NWT or Nunavut, which run their own programs.
