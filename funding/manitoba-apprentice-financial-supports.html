Financial supports for apprentices in Manitoba

Subsidized technical training

  • The province pays most of the tuition (on average $4,200 per apprentice per level). You pay a personal contribution fee.
  • From August 1, 2026 the fee is $425 for 4 weeks of training, rising by $25 a week to $575 for 10 weeks. Books and materials typically cost $800 to $1,500.

Support during technical training (case by case)

  • Lodging if training is 50+ km from home and you pay for two residences: up to $250 every two weeks (south) or $340 every two weeks (north). This can't be combined with the commuting benefit.
  • A one-time travel payment for relocating: 15 cents/km (south) or 17 cents/km (north).
  • Commuting, bus and child care supports are also available.
  • About six weeks before training you get an email with details and intake forms. Plan to pay up front in case funding is delayed or denied.
  • Federal: EI, tax deductions, and the Canada Apprentice Loan.

Bursaries and grants

  • Access to Apprenticeship Bursary: for current apprentices with financial need who face barriers to completing. Apply by May 31; awarded each June; amount varies.
  • Tim McLean Memorial Fund Bursary: for current Indigenous apprentices in technical training with financial need. Apply by May 31; awarded each June.
  • Tool Allowance and Apprenticeship Grant: for Motor Vehicle Body Repairer and Painter apprentices. Up to a $5,000 tool allowance plus $2,000 per level completed.
  • Contact: Apprenticeship Manitoba, 204-945-3337 or 1-877-978-7233.
