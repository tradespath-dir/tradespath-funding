Saskatchewan Youth Apprenticeship (SYA) Industry Scholarships

  • Run by the Saskatchewan Apprenticeship and Trade Certification Commission (SATCC) for high school students in the SYA program.
  • SYA Industry Scholarships are $1,000 each, awarded to graduating high school students who completed SYA.
  • For 2025-26, SATCC awarded 110 scholarships to students from 77 communities.
  • SYA students who register as apprentices within 5 years of graduating also get benefits toward their apprenticeship.

How to take part

  1. Ask your high school whether it offers SYA, and complete the SYA challenges.
  2. Check SATCC for current scholarship criteria and deadlines.
  3. Questions: SATCC, 1-877-363-0536, apprenticeship@gov.sk.ca.
