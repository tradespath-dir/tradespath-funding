Saskatchewan Apprenticeship Training Allowance (ATA) and other supports

From the Saskatchewan Apprenticeship and Trade Certification Commission (SATCC).

Apprenticeship Training Allowance

  • Helps apprentices who must keep a second residence while attending technical training.
  • $200 a week, or $250 a week if your main residence is in the Northern Saskatchewan Administration District.
  • Eligibility: you are eligible for or receiving Employment Insurance, or EI deems you an "insured participant"; you pay extra for a second residence at the training location; and your home is 50 km or more from the training location.
  • Apply through your MyATC account.

Employment Insurance during training

Apply online with Service Canada right after your last day of work. Your claim starts the day you apply. You don't need your Record of Employment to start. SATCC sends you the reference code you need.

Graduate Retention Program

A refundable Saskatchewan income tax credit of up to $24,000 for eligible graduates who stay in Saskatchewan and file a Saskatchewan tax return.

Federal

The Red Seal Completion Bonus, Canada Apprentice Loan, tuition tax credit and tradesperson's tools deduction. See federal apprentice supports.
