About and methodology

Trades Path Funding lists money for people taking a non-traditional route: apprentices, trades and college students, and people returning to school.

  • Every page links the official government or program source it was checked against, along with the date it was checked.
  • We list only programs that are open or announced, and we flag closed programs so readers don't chase them.
  • We don't take applications or payments, and we never charge to "find" funding. Official programs are free to apply to.
